Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

AxTrader is operated by Asterix Holdings Ltd ("we", "us"), a company registered in Ghana under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992). This policy explains what data AxTrader collects, why, and how it's handled — across our website (axtrader.vercel.app) and our Android app, which is a wrapper around the same website.

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or SMS data. AxTrader has no reason to request any of these permissions and does not.

Third parties we use

  • Supabase — authentication and account storage
  • NOWPayments — cryptocurrency payment processing (web purchases only)
  • Vercel — website hosting and server logs
  • GitHub — hosts our public trading-signal data feed (a Gist) — this feed contains no personal data, only market signal information
  • CoinGecko — public cryptocurrency price data
  • Telegram — if you choose to join our Telegram channels or contact support, Telegram's own privacy policy applies to that interaction

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policy. We only send them what's needed for them to perform their specific function — we do not sell your data to anyone, for any reason.

Your Android app specifically

The AxTrader Android app is a Trusted Web Activity — it displays the same website you'd see in a browser, inside an app shell. It does not access any Android-specific data (contacts, files, location, etc.) beyond standard internet access. All purchases happen on the website, in your browser — the app itself never collects or processes payment information.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (below) — we'll act on verified requests within 30 days.

Children

AxTrader is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of AxTrader after a change means you accept the update.
